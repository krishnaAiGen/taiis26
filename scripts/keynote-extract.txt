Keynote speakers

Amiya Nayak, IEEE Fellow, School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, University of Ottawa, Ottawa

 Dr. Amiya Nayak is a distinguished Full Professor at the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS) at the University of Ottawa, Canada. An internationally recognized leader in his field, he is a Fellow of the IEEE (FIEEE), an honor reserved for researchers with an extraordinary record of accomplishment. He holds a Ph.D. in Systems and Computer Engineering from Carleton University and a B.Math from the University of Waterloo, forming a strong academic foundation for his extensive career in technology.
Before transitioning into academia, Dr. Nayak spent over 17 years in the private sector, where he gained deep technical expertise in software engineering, avionics, and navigation systems. This extensive industrial background uniquely informs his research, allowing him to bridge the gap between theoretical computing and practical, real-world applications. His work is characterized by a focus on the reliability and security of the complex systems that power modern society.
His primary research interests lie at the intersection of distributed systems and network security. He is particularly well-known for his contributions to wireless and mobile computing, including ad hoc and sensor networks, as well as the security of the Internet of Things (IoT). Dr. Nayak’s work often explores fault-tolerant computing and decentralized access control, seeking to ensure that large-scale networks remain functional and secure even under high-stress conditions or cyber threats.
A prolific scholar, Dr. Nayak has authored or co-authored over 190 research papers and several influential books, including Wireless Sensor and Actuator Networks. With over 9,000 citations to his name, his research continues to shape the way engineers design secure, interconnected environments. Today, he continues to mentor the next generation of engineers at the University of Ottawa, leading cutting-edge projects in distributed intelligence and cybersecurity.

Yang Xiang
IEEE Fellow; Department of Software Systems and Cybersecurity, Faculty of Information Technology, Monash University, Australia

Prof. Yang Xiang is an internationally recognized researcher and academic specializing in Cybersecurity, Artificial Intelligence, Network Security and Digital Innovation. He is a Professor in the Department of Software Systems and Cybersecurity, Faculty of Information Technology, Monash University, Australia. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Deakin University in 2007.
Prior to joining Monash University in August 2026, Prof. Xiang served as Director and Dean of the Digital Research and Innovation Capability Platform at Swinburne University of Technology. In this role, he led strategic initiatives in digital transformation, blockchain technology and industry-driven research across manufacturing, healthcare, energy, transportation and finance.
With more than two decades of research experience, Prof. Xiang has published over 300 scientific papers in leading international journals and conferences, including ACM CCS, IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy, USENIX Security and NDSS. His research focuses on software and system security, network and application security, and the security of artificial intelligence throughout its development and deployment lifecycle.
Prof. Xiang has held several distinguished editorial positions, including Editor-in-Chief of a SpringerBriefs series on cybersecurity and Associate Editor of ACM Computing Surveys. He has also served on the editorial boards of leading IEEE journals, including IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing, IEEE Internet of Things Journal, IEEE Transactions on Computers and IEEE Transactions on Parallel and Distributed Systems.
He is a Fellow of the IEEE and a member of the Australian Research Council College of Experts. He has also served as Asia Coordinator of the IEEE Computer Society Technical Committee on Distributed Processing. Beyond academia, he actively contributes to industry collaboration and advises public and private organizations in Australia on cybersecurity strategy, technology risk, digital innovation and major technology initiatives.

Chih-Yang Lin, IET Fellow
Department of Mechanical Engineering at National Central University (NCU) in Taiwan

Dr. Chih-Yang Lin is a professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at National Central University (NCU) in Taiwan, where he directs the Computer Vision and Interactive Technology (CVIT) Lab.
He is an internationally recognized scholar in fields bridging computer science and mechanical systems. His expertise spans computer vision, machine learning, big data analysis, and intelligent robotics. In April 2022, he was elected as a Fellow of the Institution of Engineering and Technology (IET). He is also an IEEE Senior Member and has authored over 200 research papers in major international journals and conferences.
Before his tenure at NCU, he served as a professor at Yuan Ze University, where he also held administrative roles as Chief of Global Affairs and Dean of the International Academy. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Information Engineering from National Chung Cheng University.

Javier (Javi) Del Ser 

University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU), TECNALIA

Dr. Javier (Javi) Del Ser is a Research Professor in Artificial Intelligence at TECNALIA Research &amp; Innovation and a Distinguished Researcher at the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU).
He is an internationally recognized expert in Machine Learning, Heuristic Optimization, and Explainable AI (XAI). He has authored over 450 scientific papers with more than 33,000 citations, placing him consistently on Clarivate’s Highly Cited Researchers list and the Stanford World's Top 2% Scientist rankings. His work bridges advanced computational intelligence with real-world applications in smart grids, transport, and health, while actively shaping national AI strategies and ethical AI compliance. Dr. Del Ser holds two Ph.D. degrees (from the University of Navarra and the University of Alcalá) and is an IEEE Senior Member.

Kwok Tai Chui
 Department of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science, Hong Kong Metropolitan University (HKMU),  Hong Kong

Kwok Tai Chui received the B.Eng. degree in electronic and communication engineering - Business Intelligence Minor and Ph.D. degree from City University of Hong Kong. He had industry experience as Senior Data Scientist in Internet of Things (IoT) company. He is with the Department of Technology, School of Science and Technology, at Hong Kong Metropolitan University as an Associate Professor. He has more than 100 research publications including edited books, book chapters, journal papers, and conference papers. He has served as various editorial position in ESCI/SCIE-listed journals including Managing Editor of International Journal on Semantic Web and Information Systems, Topic Editor of Sensors, Associate Editor of International Journal of Energy Optimization and Engineering. His research interests include computational intelligence, data science, energy monitoring and management, intelligent transportation, smart metering, healthcare, machine learning algorithms and optimization.

Elhadj Benkhelifa
Department of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, Staffordshire University, UK.

Prof. Elhadj Benkhelifa is an internationally recognized academic and researcher specializing in Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Cloud and Edge Computing, the Internet of Things (IoT), and Intelligent Systems. He currently holds a strategic leadership role at Liverpool John Moores University, UK, following sixteen years at Staffordshire University, where he served as Head of the Professoriate and Founding Director of the Smart Systems, AI and Cybersecurity Research Centre.
With an extensive academic and research career, Prof. Benkhelifa has authored more than 150 scientific publications and contributed to numerous international conferences, edited volumes, and journal special issues. His research focuses on developing secure, intelligent, and resilient computing systems, with particular interests in bio-inspired computing, distributed intelligence, and data governance.
He has co-founded four international conferences and delivered nearly 40 keynote presentations at academic and industry events worldwide. He also serves on the editorial board of PeerJ Computer Science and has contributed as a guest editor for several international journals.
In recognition of his contributions to research, innovation, and industry collaboration, he was selected for Innovate UK's prestigious 50 Faces of KTP initiative in 2025. He is a Senior Member of IEEE and has served as Chair of the IEEE United Kingdom and Ireland Education and STEM office since 2022.
Prof. Benkhelifa holds a PhD in Bio-inspired Evolvable and Self-Healing Systems from the Bristol Robotics Laboratory. His academic contributions extend beyond research to doctoral supervision, international collaboration, and the advancement of interdisciplinary research in emerging computing technologies.

Dr. Yu Yuan
Former President, IEEE Standards Association

Dr. Yu Yuan is an internationally recognized researcher, inventor and entrepreneur specializing in emerging technologies, including Artificial Intelligence, Virtual and Augmented Reality, the Metaverse, the Internet of Things (IoT), Blockchain and Digital Transformation. He holds a Ph.D., an M.S. and a B.S. in Computer Science from Tsinghua University.
He previously served as a Research Scientist at IBM Research, contributing to major technological initiatives, including the Cell Broadband Engine, Smarter Planet and the Internet of Things. He subsequently founded 0xSenses Corporation, a multinational company specializing in virtual and augmented reality and human augmentation, and co-founded VerseMaker, a metaverse platform.
Dr. Yuan served as President of the IEEE Standards Association in 2023 and as a member of the IEEE Board of Directors. He has played a significant role in advancing international technology standardization, particularly in consumer technology, blockchain, virtual and augmented reality, and emerging digital ecosystems. Under his leadership, the IEEE Consumer Technology Society Standards Committee received the IEEE Standards Association Standards Committee Award in 2019. He also established IEEE's first blockchain standards committee and has chaired the IEEE Virtual and Augmented Reality Working Group, bringing together participants from more than 200 companies and institutions.
Beyond his contributions to IEEE, Dr. Yuan is the Founder and Chairman of the Globalized Intelligent Virtualized Economy (GIVE) initiative, President of the Metaverse Acceleration and Sustainability Association, and Treasurer and Board Director of the Metaverse Standards Forum. He has also held leadership positions in international organizations dedicated to advancing metaverse technologies and digital innovation.
An IEEE Senior Member, Dr. Yuan continues to contribute to global technological innovation, international standardization and industry collaboration, with a focus on developing interoperable, intelligent and sustainable digital ecosystems.
