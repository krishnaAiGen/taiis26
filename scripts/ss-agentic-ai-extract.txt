Special Session
Special Session on Trustworthy Autonomous AI Agents and Multi-Agent IoT Systems
Aim and Scope
Trustworthy autonomous AI agents and multi-agent IoT systems emphasize intelligent ecosystems where AI agents can independently perceive environments, reason over information, plan tasks, collaborate with other agents, and execute actions through connected IoT devices and cyber-physical systems. The session focuses on agentic AI, multi-agent intelligence, autonomous decision-making, large language model-based agents, edge intelligence, federated learning, digital twins, and trustworthy AI to develop reliable, transparent, secure, and accountable autonomous IoT systems.
This session provides a forum for researchers, academicians, and industry practitioners to present advanced methodologies, intelligent architectures, and real-world applications of trustworthy autonomous AI agents and multi-agent IoT systems. It encourages interdisciplinary research integrating agentic AI, multi-agent coordination, foundation models, explainable AI, edge-cloud intelligence, digital twins, and human–AI collaboration, addressing theoretical, methodological, and practical challenges in autonomous systems while emphasizing trust, safety, robustness, privacy, reliability, and human oversight.
