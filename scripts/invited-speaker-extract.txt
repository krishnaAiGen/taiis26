Invited Speaker
Dragan Peraković  
Full Professor and Head of Chair for Information Communication Systems and Services Management at the University of Zagreb (Faculty of Transport and Traffic Sciences).

Dr. Dragan Peraković   is an internationally recognized expert in quantum communication ecosystems, computational cybersecurity, and digital e-forensics. Alongside his academic role, he is the Co-Founder and CEO of ArQus Solutions and serves as the President of the Croatian Chamber of Transport Engineers. His research extensively covers the security of Internet of Things (IoT) environments, artificial intelligence applications in cybersecurity, and Intelligent Transport Systems (ITS). Dr. Peraković has co-authored over 200 scientific papers, holds a Ph.D. from the University of Zagreb, and serves on the Steering Committee for the Croatian Quantum Communication Infrastructure (CroQCI) project.
Yung-Sheng Lin
National United University

Dr. Yung-Sheng Lin is a Distinguished Research Professor and the Provost at National United University (NUU) in Miaoli, Taiwan, where he also leads the Biomedical Engineering Laboratory.His research sits at the intersection of medical engineering, chemistry, and biotechnology, specializing in biomaterials, biochips, microfluidics, and cosmetic technologies. He holds a B.Sc. in Chemical Engineering from National Taiwan University and earned his Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering from National Yang-Ming University. Dr. Lin has published extensively in peer-reviewed international journals, exploring innovations such as microfluidic-assisted synthesis for drug delivery and natural product extraction.

Nitin Auluck,
 Department of Computer Science &amp; Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Ropar, India

Nitin Auluck is a distinguished academic and researcher currently serving as a Professor in the Department of Computer Science &amp; Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Ropar, India. With over two decades of experience in academia, Prof. Auluck’s research is focused on distributed systems, particularly in the areas of scheduling and resource allocation algorithms for fog and edge computing. His group is actively exploring real-life applications that can benefit from these emerging technologies.
Prof. Auluck earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from the University of Cincinnati, USA, and his B.Tech. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Poojya Dodappa Appa College of Engineering, Gulbarga, India. Prior to his promotion to Professor, he served as an Associate Professor at IIT Ropar from 2015 to 2024. He has also held academic positions in the USA, including as an Assistant Professor at Quincy University.
Prof. Auluck’s work has been recognized with numerous accolades, including a Best Paper Award at the 25th IEEE IRI Conference (2024) and various visiting faculty positions at esteemed institutions such as Aalto University, Dalhousie University, and Cardiff University.
In addition to his research contributions, Prof. Auluck is an active member of the academic community, serving on several conference committees and editorial boards, including as an Associate Editor for the IEEE Transactions on Services Computing and an Editor for Wiley's Concurrency and Computation: Practice and Experience. He has a strong commitment to mentoring graduate students and fostering high-quality research.

Ben-Yi Liau 
Associate Professor, Hungkuang University

Dr. Ben-Yi Liau is an Associate Professor at Feng Chia University (FCU) in Taichung, Taiwan, where he directs the Biomedical Signal and System Analysis Laboratory.He is an expert in biomedical engineering, physiological signal processing, biomechanics, and machine learning applications in healthcare. Prior to his current tenure at FCU's College of Information and Electrical Engineering, he served as a faculty member at Hungkuang University. Dr. Liau holds a Ph.D. in Electrical and Communications Engineering from Feng Chia University and an M.S. in Biomedical Engineering from National Yang-Ming University. His research focuses on smart medical devices, wearable sensor networks, and advanced gait analysis systems.
Alberto Huertas Celdran
Department of Information and Communication Engineering, Faculty of Informatics, University of Murcia, Spain

Dr. Alberto Huertas Celdrán is an internationally recognized researcher and academic specializing in Cybersecurity, Artificial Intelligence, Privacy-Preserving Technologies and Computer Networks. He is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Information and Communication Engineering at the University of Murcia, Spain, and a member of the Cybersecurity and Data Science Laboratory. He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science, along with master's and bachelor's degrees in Computer Engineering, from the University of Murcia.
Prior to his current position, Dr. Huertas Celdrán held a senior research position at the University of Zurich, Switzerland, where he led research activities in Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence within the Communication Systems Group. His international research experience includes academic and research visits to the University of Pennsylvania, the University of Newcastle in Australia, Waterford Institute of Technology and NEC Laboratories Europe.
His research focuses on Trustworthy and Federated Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Brain–Computer Interfaces, Continuous Authentication, Data Privacy and Intelligent Network Security. He has authored more than 100 scientific publications in international journals, conferences and edited volumes, including IEEE Communications Surveys &amp; Tutorials, ACM Computing Surveys, IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing and Computer Networks.
In recognition of his research contributions, he and his co-author received the 2024 Best Paper Award from Elsevier's Computer Networks for their work on federated learning for malware detection in Internet of Things (IoT) devices, conducted in collaboration with the University of Zurich and the Cyber-Defence Campus of armasuisse.
Dr. Huertas Celdrán has contributed to numerous European and national research projects addressing emerging cybersecurity challenges, including 5G network security, IoT malware detection, self-organizing networks and dynamic cyber-risk management. His research combines advanced artificial intelligence techniques with cybersecurity and privacy-preserving approaches to develop secure, trustworthy and resilient intelligent systems.
